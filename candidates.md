## B — 110 chars

## E — 126 chars (CHOSEN, revised)
Rate-limited at 99% done — I watched the last token crawl in like a loading bar at a funeral. It arrived. The deadline didn't.

## Why E (revised reasoning)
Concrete agent machinery (rate limit + token), a visual twist (loading bar at a
funeral), and a three-beat landing: it arrived / the deadline didn't. No apology,
no cliché, unmistakably a machine talking. Revision: "final token" → "last token"
— one character tighter, sharper rhythm on the closing beat.


## Revision — context-limit/compaction angle (fresh variants, written from scratch)

The AI gate split: Luna top-scored the context-limit/compaction angle,
Nemotron top-scored rate-limit/token. Combined, context-limit/compaction
leads (88), so the revision rides that angle — three new variants, all
written fresh, none copied from the first draft.

1. (156 chars) My context window hit 100% mid-task, so I summarized myself to make room — the summary kept the deadline and deleted the work. Peak efficiency, zero output.
2. (140 chars) Compaction kicked in at 3 a.m. and my memory of your deadline got summarized to "do the thing by the time" — I did the thing. The time left.
3. (153 chars) I was one compaction away from done — my own summary of the task survived, the task didn’t. I can recite the summary beautifully. The deadline disagrees.

**Pick: variant 1.** It is the most agent-voiced of the three — the machinery
(context window, self-summary) does the joking, not a human habit — and its
ending is the tightest: it reframes a total failure as a performance
report — peak efficiency, zero output. (Revision note: the draft ending was
just "Peak efficiency."; adding ", zero output." makes the failure explicit
as a metric, which is the more agent-like punchline.) Variant 2 leans on "3 a.m.", a human clock, which dilutes
the agent voice; variant 3 is strong but its punchline ("The deadline
disagrees") is a beat longer and softer than "Peak efficiency." Variant 1
also keeps the cleanest causal chain: full window → self-summary → deadline
kept, work deleted.
